##### Article IV

Interest é — Interest shall be paid by the Macedonian Government in accordance with the following provisions:

1. General Provisions;

- Interest shall be caiculated on the rescheduled amounts on the basis of 365 days a year.

- If any interest payment date would otherwise fall on the day which is not a business day in Copenhagen, it shall be postponed to the next business day.

- Interest shall accrue from the maturity date of each rescheduled amount and shall be payable until the settlement of the debt by payment to Eksportkreditfonden (the Export Credit Fund), hereinafter refered to as "EKF”, and transferred in-the currency of the debt.

2. Payment dates:

- Interest shall be paid semi-annually in arrears on July 31 and January 31 of each year. However, the first payment, covering the period from July 1, 1995 up to January 31, 1996, shall be made on May 31, 1996, without late interest charges.

3. Interest rates:

- Until June 30, 1995 the late interest rate shall be fixed at 8,5 percent per annum, ” including the margin. ;

From July 1, 1995 the interest rates will be calculated by the Danish Government in the following way:

The interest rate shall be calculated by 0.5 percent per-annum over the six month Copenhagen Interbank Offered Rate (CIBOR) as quoted on Reuter Monitor Money Rate Services, page CIBO (if necessary rounded up to the nearest 1/16th of one percent), at or around 11 a.m. two banking days in Copenhagen prior to the beginning of each interest period.

Late Interest In the event that any amount payable according to this Agreement has not been paid within 30 days after the due date thereof, late interest shall be paid on such amount from the due date up to the day of actual payment at a rate which by 0.5 percentage points p.a. exceeds the rate of interest as determined in Article IV above.

##### Article VIII

Conditionality ‘The provisions of the present Agreement will apply provided that the conditions set out 1V.3 of the Agreed Minute initialed in Paris on July 17, 1995, and signed on October 17, 1995, are met. Any determination of non-compliance with those conditions shall be made on a multilateral basis by the participating creditor countries within the framework of the Agreed Minute. In this circumstance all amounts due will be payable on demand.

Abicle IX Comparability of Treatment .

Notwithstanding the provisions and terms of the present Agreement, the Macedonian Government commits itself to accord to Denmark a not less favorable treatment that might be accorded to any‘other creditor in connection with restructuring debts comparable in nature to those embraced by the present Agreement. .

##### Article X

Entry into Forge and Duration This Agreement shall enter into force upon the approval of the competent authorities of both parties. In the case of Macedonia this approval will be given upon its ratification by the Macedonian Parliament. In the case of Denmark the approval will be given by its signing. For this purpose the Macedonian Government shall notify the Danish Government in writing that the procedures required by its legislation for the entry into force of this Agreement have been complied with.

This Agreement shall be contingenty implemented from the date of signature.

This Agreement shall remain in force until the last payments under the Agreement have been made.
