##### ARTICLE 7

## TERMINATION

7.1 Termination of this TA by mutual agreement of the respective signatories may take place at any time.

7.2 Inthe absence of mutual agreement, termination of this TA by either signatory is possible at any time, provided that the cancelling party declares its intent to cancel a minimum of 3 months before the date the cancellation is to take effect. 7.3 In the event that there are financial issues or claims which remain unresolved at the time of the termination, the provisions of this Technical Arrangement shall remain in force until the specific issues are properly settled.
