##### Член 1

Се ратификува Договорот меѓу Владата на Република Македонија и Советот на министри на Република Албанија за соработка во борбата против тероризмот, организираниот криминал, недозволената трговија со наркотични дроги, психотропни супстанции и прекурсори, илегалната миграција и други нелегални активности, склучен во Скопје на 17 јуни 2004 година.

б) превенција од насилство;

в) организирање, изведување и управување со обезбедувањето на државната граница и наблњудување на копнените и морските, односно езерските делови на границата.

(4) Договорните страни ќе соработуваат во професионална основна и специјална обука и промовирање на експерти од двете договорни страни, во областите наведени во став 1 на овој член.

##### Член 5

За имплементација на овој Договор Договорните страни ќе:

а)организираат состаноци на експерти во рамите на нивните министерства за внатрешни работи и/или други институции, за областите од нивна надлежност, кога е тоа потребно да се решат итни и специјани проблеми како трговија со наркотични дроги, психотропни супстанции и прекурсори, оружје и муниција, трговија со луше, мрежи на илегална миграција и други форми на организиран криминал. б)да си помагаат една на друга, врз основа на претходна консултација, во рамките на различни релевантни форуми, организации и меѓународни тела.

##### Член 6

(1) Секоја од Договорните страни може да одбие барање за соработка или информации ако смета дека таквото барање може да го загрози суверенитетот или безбедноста на замолената Договорна страна или ако таквото исполнување е спротивно со внатрешното законодавство или со заложбите што произлегуваат од меѓународните договори. Во такви случаи, одбивањето на барањето ќе биде пренесено, без одложување, до другата Договорна страна.

(2) Информациите, обезбедени врз основа на овој Договор, ќе се сметаат за доверливи -и ќе се користат само од надлежните власти назначени во овој Договор и само за целите за кои се доставени и тие нема да се доставуваат или пренесуваат до трети држави, освен со претходно писмено одобрение од Договорната страна од која потекнуваат.

(3) Секоја Договорна страна ќе гарантира заштита на доверливите податоци добиени согласно одредбите на овој Договор, кога барањето на информациите е во целосна согласност со нејзиното внатрешно Законодавство.

(4) Секоја Договорна страна ќе има должност да избрише или уништи било кои податоци во согласност со условите наведени од Договорната страна испраќач, а ако такви услови не се договорени, веднаш штом податоците ќе престанат да бидат потребни за целите на барањето или преносот. Во случај на откажување на овој Договор, сите податоци добиени врз основа на Договорот, мора да избришат или уништат најдоцна до датумот на откажување.

##### Член 8

Трошоците поврзани со иплементација на одредбите на овој Договор како и тие поврзани со размената на делегации ќе сносат заеднички и ќе се регулираат со склучување на Протоколи согласно член 3 став 4 на овој Договор.

##### Член 9

Овој Договор ќе се склучи на неопределено време и може да биде откажан или суспендиран во секое време по пат на писмено известување доставено до другата Договорна страна по дипломатски пат. Откажувањето ќе влезе во сила во рок од 6 (шест) месеци, а суспензијата во рок од 30 (триесет) дена од денот на известувањето.

##### Член 10

Овој Договор ќе влезе во сила на датумот на последното известување разменето по дипломатски пат во врска со исполнувањето на внатрешните правни постапки за неговото влегување во сила. Составено во Скопје, Република Македонија, на 17-ти јуни во 2004 година, во два оригинални примерока, секој на македонски, албански и англиски јазик, при што секој текст е со еднаква важност. Во случај на несогласување во интерпретацијата англискиот текст е одлучувачки.

## "AGREEMENT

between the Government of Republic of Macedonia and the Counsil of ministers of Republic of Albania on the cooperation in combating terrorism, organized crime, illicit trafficking in narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, and precursors, illegal migration and other illegal activities The Government of Republic of Macedonia and the Counsil of ministers of Republic of Albania hereinafter referred to as Contracting Parties, In the spirit of the existing friendship and cooperation between the two countries, Aware of the necessity to protect the life, property, fundamental rights and other freedoms of their own nationals, Recognizing the advantages of the international cooperation, as a factor of utmost importance in effectively preventing and combating organized crime, terrorism, illicit trafficking in narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, and precursors, illegal migration and other illegal activities, Taking into account the provisions of international conventions and treaties in the field, to which the states of the Contracting Parties are parties, Have agreed as follows:

##### Article 1

(1) The Contracting Parties, according to their legislation, shall cooperate and mutual assist each other in prevention and identification of crimes in the following fields:

(a) terrorism;

(b) organized crime; wi (c) illicit production and release for trade of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances and precursors;

(d) trafficking in human beings and human organs, and exploitation of children; (e) extortion;

 (f) illicit production, trade and possession of firearms, ammunition and explosives, chemichal, biological, nuclear and radioactive materials, goods and technology with double use, army technique, materials and equipment and components that can be used for production of weapons for mass destruction and other dangerous materials;

(g) mediation in prostitution;

(h) theft and illicit trade in cultural and historical values, artistic values, objects of cultural and historical value, precious metals and minerals and other objects of larger value;

(i) counterfeiting money, securities and marks of value, means of payment without cash which are not securities, as well as their releasing in circilation or use; (i) organizing illegal games of chance and misrepresentation used in legal games of chance;

(k) smuggling of excise goods and other crimes related to international trade and + economy exchange, tax evasion, money laudering and other unlawful property gain;

(I) theft and illegal trade of vehicles, counterfeiting and use of counterfeith documents for those vehicles;

(m) illegal migration-illegal transfer of persons accross the state border, counterfeiting and use of counterfeith passports and visas;

(n) crimes against life and human health;

(0) crimes against the environment.

(2) With mutual agreement, the Contracting Parties shall mutual assist each other also in fight against other crimes . The Contracting Parties shall assist each other only for activities that are considered as crimes according to national legislation. ye

(3) The Contracting Parties shall cooperate and mutual assist each other also in the fields of: !

a) methods and means used to ensure, maintain and re-establish public order, to safeguard and protect targets of public and national interest, values and goods, as well as to guarantee the security of the persons who take part of the public demonstrations;

b) prevention of violence;

¢) organizing, performing and managing State border guard and surveillance on land and sea or lake sectors.

(4) The Contracting Parties shall cooperate in professional basic and special training and promoting of the experts of the two Contracting Parties, in the fields mentioned in paragraph 1 of this Article.

##### Article 2

(1) The two Contracting Parties, according to their legislation, shall cooperate by means of:

a) exchanging information in the fields defined in Article 1 of the present Agreement;

b) taking the steps or actions agreed upon a mutual basis by the ministries and/or other competent bodies of the two states;

c) exchanging of experts specialized in areas of mutual interest;

d) exchanging of documentation, publications and results of scientific research carried out in areas of mutual interest;

e) exchanging of operational information to help preventing, identifying and investigating organized crime activities and other crimes.

(2) The Contracting Parties shall agree upon, after the fulfilment of the legal procedures in force in their states, on mutual agreement, to send liaison officers designed by the Competent Authorities mentioned in Article 3 of the present Agreement.

##### Article 3

(1) To implement the provisions of the present Agreement, the Contracting Parties designate the following as Competent Authorities:

For the Macedonian Party:

a) Ministry of Internal Affairs For the Albanian Party:

a) The Ministry of Public Order;

(2) In order to ensure the operational link, the Competent Authorities shall designate a contact person, and the Contracting Parties shall notify each other, by diplomatic channels, on the particulars of this person, i.e. name, surname, position, telephone, fax, within 30 days after the entry into force of the present Agreement.

(3) The cooperation between the Competent Authorities of the Contracting Parties, in accordance with the provisions of the present Agreement, shall carry out in English language or any other language agreed upon, on mutual agreement, by the Contracting Parties.

(4) In order to implement the cooperation, in accordance with the provisions of Article 1 and Article 2 of the present Agreement, the Competent Authorities of the Contracting Parties, under their legally established competencies, may conclude cooperation protocols, with due observance of the internal legislation of the two states.

##### Article 4

The Contracting Parties shall assist each other in processing and analyzing information pertaining to the fields mentioned in Article 1 of the present Agreement and may furnish equipment and specific technology to the Competent Authorities of the other Contracting Party, under the international commitments undertaken in this field.

##### Article 5

For the implementation of the present Agreement the Contracting Parties shall:

a) Organize meetings of the experts within their Ministries of interior and/or other institutions, for their relevant areas of competence, whenever is necessary to solve urgent and special problems such as trafficking in narcotic drugs, psychotropic substance, and precursors, firearms and ammunition, trafficking in human beings, illegal migration networks or other forms of organized crime.

b) Assist each other, on the basis of prior consultation, within the various relevant fora, organizations and international bodies.

##### Article 6

(1) Each Contracting Party may deny a request for cooperation or information if it considers that such a request might threaten the sovereignty or seourity of the Requested Contracting Party, or if such fulfilling may contradict its national legislation or the commitments arising from international agreements. In such cases, the denial of the request shall be notified, without delay, to the other Contracting Party.

(2) The information, provided on the basis of the present Agreement, shall be considered confidential and be used only by the Competent Authorities designed in this Agreement and only for the purposes of their exchange and they shall not be communicated or transferred to a third state, except with the prior written approval of the originating Contracting Party.

(3) Each Contracting Party shall guarantee the protection of the classified data received in accordance with the provisions of the present Agreement, as the request of information in full compliance with its national legislation.

(4) Each Contracting Party shall have the duty to delete or destroy any data in accordance with the conditions stipulated by the transmitting Contracting Party or, if such conditions have not been settled, as soon as they cease to be needed for the purpose of the request or transmission. In case of termination of this Agreement all data received on its basis, must be deleted or destroyed at the latest by the termination date.

16

##### Article 7

(1) The provisions of the present Agreement shall not affect the rights and undertakings derived for the Contracting Parties from other bilateral or multilateral international conventions or agreements.

(2) Any disputes concerning the interpretation or application of the present Agreement shall be settled by way of consultations between the Contracting Parties.

##### Article 8

The expenses related to the implementation of the provisions of the present Agreement as well as those connected to the exchange of delegations shall be borne on a mutual basis and shall be regulated upon concluding the Protocols according to Article 3 paragraph 4 of the present Agreement.

##### Article 9

This Agreement shall be concluded for and indefinite period of time and can be denounced or suspended at any time by written notification addressed to the other Contracting Party through diplomatic channels. The denunciation will take effect within 6 (six) months while the suspension within 30 (thirty) days from the date of notification.

##### Article 10

This Agreement shall enter into force on the date of the last notification exchanged through diplomatic channels regarding the fulfillment of the internal legal procedures necessary for its entry into force.
