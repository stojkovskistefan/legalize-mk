(A) The Republic of Albania, the Republic of Bulgaria and the Republic of Macedonia are signatories to the Energy Charter Treaty;

(B) The States wish to cooperate in facilitating the development, construction and operation of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline for the carriage of Petroleum in and across their Territories in accordance with the principles of the Tripartite Memorandum of Understanding with respect to the Project signed by States on December 28, 2004; and (C) The States now desire to enter into this Convention to provide more detail with respect to the understandings reached by the States in such Tripartite Memorandum of Understanding, to provide security and protection to investment in the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline and to safeguard the efficient and secure development, ownership and operation of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline within their Territories, all in accordance with this Convention, the Bilateral Agreements, the Energy Charter Treaty and the relevant Project Agreements.

## 2. GENERAL OBLIGATIONS AND COMMITMENTS

2.1. Timely Performance From and after the Effective Date, each State shail at ail times fulfill and perform on a umely basis each of its duties and obligations arising under this Convention and under any Bilateral Agreement it has entered into.

2.2. Responsibility Each State shall observe all provisions of this Convention and any Bilateral Agreement it has entered into and shall take all lawful measures as may be available to it to ensure complete compliance with this Convention and with any Bilateral Agreement it has entered into and with any Project Agreement entered into by any Relevant Authority within its Territory

## 3. TRANSIT

Whenever a situation of Transit exists, each State shall ensure the Transit of Petroleum through, into, across and from its Territory through the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline and shall take all reasonable measures and actions, including those set forth in the Energy Charter Treaty, this Convention and the Bilateral Agreement entered into by such State, to satisfy such obligation.

## 4, TITLE TO OR OWNERSHIP OF PETROLEUM

Title to or ownership of any Petroleum transported through the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline shall remain vested in the applicable Project Company(ies), Project Investor(s) and/or Shipper(s) in accordance with their commercial agreements.

## 5. UNINTERRUPTED TRANSIT OF PETROLEUM

5.1. No Interruption Each State shall not, except as specifically provided in the Bilateral Agreement it has entered into, interrupt, curtail, subject to conditions or otherwise impede the flow of Petroleum through the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in, across and/or exiting from its Territory and shall take any actions which may be necessary to prevent such interruption, curtailment or other impediment.

5.2. Notice If a State takes, fails to take or suffers the taking of any action which interrupts, curtails, subjects to conditions or otherwise impedes the flow of Petroleum through the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in its Territory, such State shall immediately give notice to the other States and relevant ~ Project Companies of any such interruption, curtailment, conditions or other impediment, the reasons therefor, and the timetable for alleviating the same, and, in the case of any such event, such State shall eliminate the reasons underlying such interruption, curtailment, conditions or other impediment and restore the uninterrupted, uncurtailed, unconditioned and unimpeded flow of Petroleum through the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in its Territory at the earliest opportunity.

## 6. STANDARDS

6.1.Cooperation Each State shall cooperate with the other States and the Project Companies through the inclusion of provisions in the Bilateral Agreement it enters into regarding the designation, formulation and establishment of compatible and internationally acceptable technical, health, quality, environment and safety standards to be observed while implementing Project Activities relating to the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline, including energy flows and associated storages, that are in accordance with generally accepted international standards and business practices within the petroleum industry and the Acquis Communautaire. Such standards as set forth in the Bilateral Agreement entered into by a State shall be appropriate to the conditions and environment prevailing in each particular geographic area traversed by the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline and shall, to the maximum extent possible after taking into account the relevant provisions of the national laws, rules, regulations and decrees of each State, be harmonized with the comparable standards set forth in the Bilateral Agreements entered into by the other States. 6.2.Consultation Each State shall consult with the other States and take all necessary steps to ensure compliance with such compatible and intemationally acceptable technical, health, quality, environment and safety standards formulated, established and harmonized in accordance with this Article and laid down in the Bilateral Agreement entered into by such State. 6.3.Spillage In the event of spillage of Petroleum from any part of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline, or any other occurrence causing or likely to cause material environmental damage or risk to health and safety, then, at the request of the affected State and without prejudice to the obligations of any Project Company under any Bilateral Agreement entered into by such State, the other States shall assist the affected State immediately and to the greatest extent possible as requested by such affected State. The Joint Commission shall determine whether the affected State shall be obligated to reimburse the other States for any Loss incurred in providing such assistance after taking into account the obligations of any Project Company under any Bilateral Agreement entered into by such affected State.

## 7. TAXES

7.1. Tax Treatment S8oa31 ~ } Each State shall ensure that the tax treatment of any Project Participant shall be no less favorable than that applicable to domestic entrepreneurs under the generally applicable tax legislation in similar circumstances, 7.2. Cooperation With respect to measures regarding Taxes, each State shall cooperate with the other States to ensure a fair and transparent application of Taxes on any Project Participant and to avoid double taxation.

7.3. Transfer Pricing .

With regard to the computation of Taxes, the principles of transfer pricing contained in the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises and Tax Administrations shall apply.

7.4. Petroleum The States shall not levy any Taxes on the value of the Petroleum that is transported through the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline.

## 8. CUSTOMS DUTIES

Each State shall accord imported goods and services associated, directly or indirectly, with any Project Activity treatment no less favorable than that accorded to like goods and services of national or foreign origin and shall otherwise, as a member of the World Trade Organization (WTO), apply WTO rules to imports of energy related materials and products required for the construction, operation and maintenance of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline, including, without limitation, pipeline equipment.

## 9. PROJECT IMPLEMENTATION

Each State shall ensure its full support for the implementation and conduct of the Project and each Project Activity as provided by this Convention and in the Bilateral Agreement entered into by such State and, in furtherance thereof, shall take all necessary steps to assure compliance by any Relevant Authority with all obligations imposed on such Relevant Authority by virtue of this Convention and the Bilateral Agreement entered into by such State or by virtue of any Project Agreement entered into by such Relevant Authority.

## 10. COOPERATION

10.1. Favorable Conditions The States shall cooperate in order to establish and maintain necessary and favorable conditions for the construction, ownership and operation by the Project Companies of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline, and for the Transit of Petroleum in and/or across their Territories. 10.2. Other Conventions or Agreements Each State shall ensure its full cooperation in the conduct of negotiating and entering into such other multilateral or bilateral conventions or agreements as may be appropriate between and among the States and other states, international institutions and authorities, including full consultation with the Project Companies, in order to authorize, enable and support the implementation of the Project and all Project Activities.

## 11. RIGHTS TO LAND AND TERRITORIAL WATERS

11.1. Land Each State shall ensure it grants exclusive Land Rights in its Territory, and maintains access thereto, for all Project Activities under fair, transparent and clear commercial terms and conditions as provided in the Bilateral Agreement entered into by such State and after taking into account the relevant provisions of the national laws, rules, regulations and decrees of such State. 11.2. Territorial Waters Each State shall ensure it grants and maintains access to the sea ports and territorial waters of such State for all Project Activities under fair, transparent and clear commercial terms and conditions as provided in the Bilateral Agreement entered into by such State.

## 12. JOINT COMMISSION

12.1. Establishment A commission shall be established consisting of two representatives from each State (a) to oversee compliance with and to facilitate the application, implementation and interpretation of this Convention and the Project, (b) to facilitate and allow the uniform and efficient operation of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline and (c) to resolve in good faith any complications, issues, problems or disputes that may arise in connection with this Convention or the Project (the “Joint Commission”). Each Project Company shall also provide two observers to assist in the work of the Joint Commission.

12.2. State Representatives Within sixty (60) days after the Effective Date, each State shall designate in writing to the other States its initial representatives on the Joint Commission. The representatives of a State shall be fully authorized and empowered by such State to act on its behalf with regard to any 100131 ~ } matter properly brought before the Joint Commission in respect of the Project. Each State shall provide notice in writing to the Project Companies of its current representatives to the Joint Commission. Each State may change its representatives effective upon delivery of written notice 10 the other States.

12.3. Project Company Representatives The Bilateral Agreement entered into by each State shall provide that the Project Company(ies) counterparty to such Bilateral Agreement shall designate in writing to such State its initial two observers on the Joint Commission. The observers shall be fully authorized and empowered by such Project Company(ies) to act on its behalf with regard to any matter properly brought before the Joint Commission in respect of the Project. Such Project Company(ies) may change its observers effective upon delivery of written notice to the States and to the other Project Companies.

12.4. Rules of Procedure The Joint Commission shall unanimously adopt rules of procedure relating to the meetings, activities and work of the Joint Commission.

## 13. BOUNDARY OR TERRITORIAL DISPUTES

Each State agrees that (a) its commitment to the Project exists independently of any dispute, difference, claim, demarcation, fixing, change or other modification regarding the location of any of its boundaries or the composition or extent of its Territory that may exist now or may arise in the future or take place between or among it and any other State(s) (for the purposes of this Article, a “Boundary or Territorial Dispute”), (b) no Boundary or Territorial Dispute between or among any of the States shall interfere in any manner with any Project Activity and (c) in particular, the obligations described in this Convention shall not be altered or varied because of a Boundary or Territorial Dispute that may arise after the Effective Date.

## 14. EXPROPRIATION

The provisions of Article 13 of the Energy Charter Treaty shall apply mutatis mutandis to this Convention and to all aspects of the Project.

## 15. BILATERAL AGREEMENTS

Each State hereby undertakes to enter into a Bilateral Agreement with the relevant Project Company(ies). Each such Bilateral Agreement shall be based upon international standards and practices, shall adhere to the extent possible to guidelines established by the Energy Charter Treaty and shall, among other things:

(a) grant to such Project Company(ies), after taking into account the relevant provisions of the national laws, rules, regulations and decrees of such State and this Convention, an exclusive right (whether by concession, franchise or other appropriate means) to design, 110131 ~ } enginecr, construct, operate and maintain the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in the Territory of such State for an agreed period of years, with agreed extension rights;

(b)specify the parameters for the design, engineering, construction, operation and maintenance of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in the Territory of such State, it being understood that the Project Company(ies) counterparty to such Bilateral Agreement will select Contractors and Shippers with whom to enter into detailed contracts for the design, engineering, construction, operation and maintenance of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in the Territory of such State; (c) estabiish the rights and obiigations of the Project Company(ies) counterparty to such Bilateral Agreement with respect to the land and rights of way used for the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in the Territory of such State;

(d) specify force majeure events and establish the rights and obligations of such State and such Project Company(ies) upon the occurrence of a force majeure event;

(e) specify events of termination and establish the rights and obligations of such State and such Project Company(ies) upon any termination of such Bilateral Agreement;

(f) provide for the settiement of disputes under such Bilateral Agreement through international arbitration;

(g) establish rules for the ownership and regulation of the interconnection of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline at the borders between States and for the offtake by such State of Petroleum for its domestic requirements;

(h) establish (i) tariffs to be charged for the Transit of Petroleum through its Territory, which tariffs may be different depending upon the circumstances under which they are charged (for example, different tariffs may be charged under long term supply contracts than under tariffs for spot market transactions), (ii) fees related to the use of sea ports, (iii) rules and procedures for the modification of such tariffs and fees and (iv) procedures to resolve disputes with respect thereto;

(i) permit the Project Company(ies) counterparty to such Bilateral Agreement to finance the Project through joint loan and credit facilities and the cross-collateralization of the assets of the Project to secure such facilities, in each case in accordance with the national laws, rules, regulations and decrees of such State;

(j) permit assets and rights of the Project Companies and the Project Investors, including, without limitation, the equity interests of any Project Investor in any Project Company, 10 be mortgaged, pledged and/or assigned to lenders as collateral for the repayment of loans and credits extended by such lenders to finance the Project and permit the Project Companies to deposit revenues and proceeds from the Project in bank accounts established outside the Territory of such State as part of security arrangements in favor of such lenders, in each case in accordance with the national laws, rules, regulations and decrees of such State;

(k) require the Project Company(ies) to construct and operate the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in accordance with all applicable environmental laws, rules, regulations and decrees; 120231 ~ }

(1) specify the types of insurance and coverage amounts to be maintained by the Project Company(ies) counterparty to such Bilateral Agreement and permit such Project Company(ies) 1o obtain such insurance from reputable local or international insurance companies meeting agreed financial criteria, (m) establish the obligations of such State with respect to the provision and maintenance of roads, utilities and other infrastructure facilities required in connection with the construction, operation and maintenance of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in the Territory of such State; and (n) establish the terms and conditions on which the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline in the Territory of such State shall be transferred to such State upon the expiration of the term of such Bilateral Agreement.

15.2. Uniform Provisions Each State agrees that the Bilateral Agreement to be executed by it shall, after taking into account the relevant provisions of its national laws, rules, regulations and decrees, be as similar as possible in form and substance to the Bilateral Agreement to be executed by each of the other States, it being the intention of the States to ensure that, to the maximum extent possible, the Project will be treated substantively as if located in only one country and as if undertaken by a single Entity, even though the Project will be located in the Territories of three States and be implemented by more than one Project Company.

## 16. LEGAL STABILITY

Each State agrees that it shall not take any action with respect to the Project or any Project Participant (including, without limitation, the modification, revocation or adoption of any law, rule, regulation or decree or the modification, revocation or withdrawal of any permit, approval or authorization in force at the time any Project Participant shall commence work on the Project) that solely, directly and uniquely applies to the Project or any Project Participant and could reasonably be expected to have a material adverse affect on the development, implementation or operation of the Project or on the legal, economic or commercial position of such Project Participant.

## 17. FOREIGN CURRENCY

The States agree that the Project Companies shall be entitled to repatriate in freely convertible foreign currency the capital and profits from investments in the Project and shall have the right to pay principal, interest, fees and other charges in respect of the debt financing of the Project in freely convertible foreign currency.

## 18. PURPOSE

The States agree that (a) the Project shall not involve the provision of goods or services to the public at large in the Territories for the purpose of satisfying any general or common need of the populace of the States or any of them, (b) the Project is not intended or required to operate in the service of any public benefit or interest in the Territories or any of them. (c) neither the Transport nor the Transit of Petroleum through the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline shall involve a ~ } common carrier and (d) accordingly, no Bilateral Agreement shall be characterized or treated, in whole or in pan, as a contract granting a concession or franchise to operate a public utility or service or in any way require a public tender for works, goods or services constituting all or any part of the Project.

## 19. DISPUTE SETTLEMENT

The States shall endeavor to settle disputes concerning the application or interpretation of this Convention through the Joint Commission. If, in the sole discretion of a State, and regardless of the status of consultations undertaken by the joint Commission, a dispute has not been settled, that State may, upon written notice to the other State(s), submit the matter for final and binding resolution to an ad hoc tribunal. Such ad hoc tribunal shall be constituted and shall conduct its proceedings in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions contained in Article 27(3) of the Energy Charter Treaty, applying such dispute resolution provisions mutatis mutandis to this Convention.

## 20. TERMINATION

20.1. Bilateral Agreements This Convention shall terminate upon the termination or expiration of all Bilateral Agreements and all Project Agreements and the conclusion of all Project Activities thereunder or otherwise related thereto.

20.2. Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline This Convention shall terminate if the construction of the Trans Balkan Oil Pipeline shall not have commenced on or before the third anniversary of the date on which all States shall have entered into their respective Bilateral Agreements, and such Bilateral Agreements shall have entered into force in accordance with the national laws, rules, regulations and decrees of the respective States.

This Convention may also be terminated by the States if the terms and conditions relating to the construction of the Trans Balkan Pipeline, including the schedules for such construction, contained in the Bilateral Agreements are not fulfilled by the Project Companies that are party thereto in accordance with the terms of the Bilateral Agreements.

## 21. TRIPARTITE MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

The States hereby acknowledge and agree that the confirmations set forth in Section 1 through Section 4 of the Tripartite Memorandum of Understanding referred to in Recital (B) of this Convention shall remain valid and in full force and effect.

## 22. EXECUTION

Four (4) original copies of this Convention in English shall be executed by each State.

## 23 ДЕПОЗИТАР, РАТИФИКАЦИЈА И ВЛЕГУВАЊЕ ВО СИЛА

23.1. Депозитар Република Македонија ќе има функција на депозитар на копијата од Конвенцијата од секоја држава и копијата на сите ратификациони документи усвоени од секоја држава.

23.2 Ратификација Оваа Конвенција не се ратификува од страна на секоја Држава во согласност со националното законодавств, правила, регулативи и одлуки. Секоја држава треба да ја извести Република Македонија, како држава Депозитар за датумот на кој државата ги  комплетирала сите барања согласно своето национално законодавство, правила, регулативи и одлуки за оваа Конвенција да влезе во сила.

23.3 Влегување во сила Оваа конвенција ќе влезе на сила на првиот ден од вториот календарски месец по датумот на кој последната држава ја известила Република Македонија, како земја Депозитар дека таа држава ја ратификувала Конвенцијата во согласност со своето националното законодавство, правила, регулативи и одлуки.

## 24 АМАНДМАНИ

Оваа Конвенција може да се промени и дополни од страна на Државите со подготовка на писмен документ од страна на секоја Држава во кој се даваат условите на таа измена, која ќе биде ратификувана и ќе стапи во сила во согласност со процедурите дадени во член 23 од оваа Конвенција.
