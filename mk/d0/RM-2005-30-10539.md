## AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF MACEDONIA AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF ALBANIA ON ESTABLISHING THE INTERNATIONAL LAKE PASSANGERS’ LINE OHRID - PODGRADEC

The Government of the Republic of Macedonia and the Government of the Republic of Albania hereinafter refereed to as "parties" Desiring to promote mutual co-operation in the field of transport, and in particular the transport of passengers on the Lake Ohrid.

Have agreed:

##### Article 1

Both parties agree to start and operate an international seasonal passengers’ line for tourists, to operate between Ohrid and Pogradec, with border crossings in Sveti Naum and Tushemisht.

Both parties agree that they shall start and operate the line on the basis of reciprocity, and in accordance with their respective national legislation,

##### Article 2

Both parties agree that the line shall be operated by the subjects registered in accordance with their respective national legislation. 

##### Article 3

Both parties have agreed that the nationals of each party, bearers of valid passports, shall enter and leave the territory of the other country on the designated border crossings with a visa in accordance with the established visa-regime between the two countries.

The nationals of one party, that are staying on the territory of the other party or intend to stay at the territory of the other party for a longer period than what has been provided with the established mutual visa-regime, are obliged to apply to the respective authorities for issue of visas or permit to stay.

The nationals of one party, during their stay on the territory of the other party are obliged to comply with the respective laws and regulations of that party.

Each party reserves its right to refuse the entry on its territory, or to cancel the stay on its territory, to a national of the other party whom it considers as a "non grata",

##### Article 4

During the period of sailing from the border crossings to the state border of both parties:

- The vessel shall not have any contact with other vessels than the vessels of police, customs and the safety service ships;

- Any loading and unloading of passengers and goods from the vessel is forbidden.

##### Article 5

The commander of the vessel is obliged to present to the customs authorities the list of goods and provisions for the needs of the operation of the vessel, the crew members and the passengers, and to the border authorities the list of the members of the crew members and list of passengers.

9 The passengers shall not be allowed to carry goods of commercial use, and arc obliged to declare to the customs authorities their personal baggage and goods which are not of commercial use.

##### Article 6

The competent authorities shall provide all necessary conditions for mutual exchange of information.

##### Article 7

The number and time table of daily trips of the vessels shall be in a separate "Plans of sailing trips" that will be defined by the operators of both countries and transmitted for approval and approved by the respective competent authorities of either party, based on its national legislation.

The respective competent authorities of each party shall inform themselves in written form about the approved “Plans of sailing trips.

##### Article 8

The operators of both parties shall respect the laws and regulations of the other party regarding the tax regime. The concrete regime tax payment shall be defined with agreement between the competent authorities of both parties.

##### Article 9

The vessels which shall operate the line, are obliged to have on board all necessary documents for the identity and safety of the vessel. 10

##### Article 10

The present Agreement shall be amended by mutual consent of the both parties.

##### Article 11

The present Agreement shall enter into force first day after the day when the two parties have informed each other, through diplomatic channels that the respective national legislative procedures of each party for its entry into force had been fulfilled.

The present Agreement is concluded for a period of 5 years from the day of its entrance into force. It shall continue to be in force for successive period of years unless it is denounced by one of the parties in written form six months before the expire of cach valid period, through diplomatic channels.

This Agreement shall be provisionally applicable from the day of signature pending entry into force.

Signed at Obeid on 5. 27 1999 in two original copies in Macedonian, Albanian and English language, both being equally authentic. In case of disagreement, the English version shall prevail.
