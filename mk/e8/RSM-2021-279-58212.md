##### Член 8

Измени и спорови Овој Меморандум за разбирање ќе може да се измени во секое време со взаемна согласност на Страните дадена во писмена форма. Овие измени ќе влезат во сила согласно член 9 од овој Меморандум за разбирање.

Секој спор кој може да произлезе од толкувањето или спроведувањето на овојМеморанлум за разбирање, ќе се решава помеѓу Страните со преговори.

##### Член 9

Влегување во сила Овој Меморандум за разбирање ќе стапи во сила на денот на прием на последното известување во писмена форма со кое Страните се известуваат меѓусебно, преку дипломатски кавали, за завршувањето на нивните интерни правни процедури кои се потребни за стапување во сила па предметниот документ.

Овој Меморандум за разбирање ќе има важност во времетраење од 5 години. Ќе се обновува автоматски за последователен период од 5 години, освен ако една од Страните не ја извести другата Страна во писмена форма и преку дипломатски канали за својата намера да го поништи Меморандумот за разбирање 6 месеци пред неговото истекување. Секоја Странка може да го раскине овој Меморандум за разбирање во кое било време. со писмено известување до пругата Страна преку дипломатски канали. Во овој случај. овој Меморандум за разбирање ќе биде раскинат два (2) месеци по датумот на приемот на известувањето.

Раскинувањето на овој Меморандум за разбирање нема да влијае на валидноста и времетраењето на активностите договорени во согласност со овој Меморандум за разбирање и веќе започнати пред тој да биде раскинат.

## MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF NORTH MACEDONIA AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF TURKEY ON COCPERATION IN THE FIELD OF FORESTRY

The Government of the Republic of North Macedonia and the Government of the Republic of Turkey (hereinafter collectively referred to as the “Parties™), Within the framework of this Memorandum of Understanding, Expressing their desire to strengthen friendly relations between the Macedonian and Turkish people and improve cooperation in the field of forestry, Taking into account the benefits and common interests that would arise from closer cooperation in the field of forestry, Have agreed as follows:

##### Article 1

Purpose For the protection. development, and management of forest resources, the Parties shall cooperate through the sharing of information, experience, and technology on the basis of equality, reciprocity, and mutual benefit under their domestic laws and legislation.

##### Article 2

Areas of Cooperation The Parties shall cooperate in the following fields:

a. Sustainable management of forests, taking into account various benefits; b. Forestry and harvesting techniques, forest products industry, sales and marketing opportunities; .

¢. Determining the value of forest services and green economy;

d. Fuelwood production and efficient clean encrgy production from wood:

e. Forest inventory;

f. Inventory of non-wood forest products and improving their management and marketing opportunities;

g. Utilizing of forests for recreational and socio-cultural purposes, urban forests; 10 04 14 h. Ecotourism;

i. Increasing the benefits of forests on water retention and soil conservation; j. Fighting against forest fires, diseases, insects, and illegal logging;

k. Supporting people, within the scope of forest management, whose livelihoods are based on the forest;

l. Watershed rehabilitation, afforestation, scedlings and seeds cultivation techniques, and combating soil erosion;

##### Article 3

Methods of Cooperation The Parties shall cooperate on the areas listed in Article 2 of this Memorandum of Understanding in the following ways:

a. Exchange of scientific and technical information and documents, b. Exchange of experts, researchers. consultants. and personnel, ¢. Organization of joint workshops, meetings, seminars, training programs, and study tours, d, Preparation and implementation of joint projects.

##### Article 4

Competent Authorities The competent authorities responsible for the implementation of this Memorandum of Understanding hereby are stated below:

1. “Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry; on behalf of the Government of the Republic of Turkey,

2. “Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Water Economy"; on behalf of the Government of the Republic of North Macedonia.

##### Article 5

Joint Committee Within a period of three (3) months after signing this Memorandum of Understanding, the Parties shall establish a Joint Comnittee composed of three (3) members from each Party representing competent Government authorities for the areas of cooperation stipulated in Article 2. The Committee shall hold regular meetings at least once a year, alternatively in the Republic of North Macedonia and in the Republic of Turkey to facilitate the implementation of this Memorandum of Understanding and performing the activities under this scope in a proper manner. The Joint Committee shall undertake the below mentioned responsibilities;

a. To review and evaluate the implementation of this Memorandum of Understanding, b. To discuss and review the cooperation activities under this Memorandum of Understanding, ¢. To negotiate other matters related to this Memorandum of Understanding.

##### Article 6

Intellectual Property Rights The Parties. in accordance with the respective national legislation and international treaties to which they are parties, shall ensure the effective protection of the rights for intellectual property arising out of this Memorandum of Understanding or to be transferred in respect thereof, In the context of this Memorandum of Understanding, the concept of intellectual property shall be construed in line with the definition specified in Article 2 of the Convention Establishing the World Intellectual Property Organization, done at Stockholm on July 14, 1967.

##### Article 7

Financial Matters The costs incurred by the cooperation activities under this Memorandum of Understanding shall be bome by the “Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Water Economy” of the Republic of North Macedonia and by the “Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry™ of the Republic of Turkey, as mutually decided on a case-by-case basis by the Parties.

##### Article 8

Amendments and Disputes This Memorandum of Understanding shall be amended by mutual written consent of the Parties at any time. These amendments shall enter into force according to the procedure specified in Article 9 of this Memorandum of Understanding.

Any dispute that may arise from the interpretation or implementation of this Memorandum of Understanding shail be resolved by the Parties through negotiation.

Article 9 .

Entry into Force This Memorandum of Understanding shall enter into force on the date of the receipt of the last written notification by which the Parties notify each other, through diplomatic channels, of the completion of their internal legal procedures required for the entry into force of the concerned document.

This Memorandum of Understanding shall remain in force for a period of five (5) years. It shall be renewed automatically for successive periods of five (3) years unless one of the Parties notifies the other in writing through diplomatic channels of its intention fo terminate this Memorandum of Understanding six (6) months prior to its expiration.

Either Party may terminate this Memorandum of Understanding at any time by giving the other Party a written notification to that effect through diplomatic channels. In this case, this Memorandum of Understanding shall be terminated two (2) months after the date of the receipt of the notification.
