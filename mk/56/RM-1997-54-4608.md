Article 1. Objectives ‘The objectives of this Convention, to be pursued in accordance with its relevant provisions, are the conservation of biological diversity, the sustainable use of its components and the fair and equitable sharing of the benefits arising out of the utilization of genetic resources, including by appropriate access to genetic resources and by appropriate transfer of relevant technologies, waking into account all rights over those resources and to technologies, and by appropriate funding. ‘ Article 2. Use of Terms For the purposes of this Convention:

"Biological diversity" means the variability among living organisms from all sources including, mter alia, terrestrial, marine and other aquatic ecosystems and the ecological complexes of which they are part; this includes diversity within species, between species and of ecosystems. "Biolugical resources" includes genetic resources, organisms or parts thereof, populations, or any other biotic component of ecosystems with actual or potential use or value for humanity. "Biotechnology" means any technological application that uses biological systems, living organisms, or derivatives thereof, to make or modify products or processes for specific use. "Country of origin of genetic resources” means the country which possesses those genetic resources, in in-situ conditions.

"County providing genetic resources” means the country supplying genetic resources collected from i a-situ sources, including populations of both wild and domesticated species, or taken trom ex-si'.u sources, which may or may not have originated in that country.

"D omesticated or cultivated species” means species in which the evolutionary process has been ir.fluenced by humans to meet their needs.

"Ecosystem" means a dynamic complex of plant, animal and micro-otganism communities and their non-living environment interacting as a functional unit.

"Sustainable use" means the use of components of biological divessity in a way and ata rate that does not lead to the long-term decline of biological diversity, thereby maintaining its potential to meet the needs and aspirations of present and future generations.

"Technology" includes biotechnology.

Комисијата за помирување своите одлуки ќе ги донесува со мнозинство гласови на своите членови. Освен доколку страните во спорот поинаку не се договорат, Комисијата сама ќе ја одреди процедурата за работа. Таа ќе донесува предлози за решавање на проблемот, кои страните во спорот ќе ги разгледаат доброволно.

##### Член 6

Недоразбирањата околу компетенциите на Комисијата за помирување ќе бидат решени од самата Комисија.

Член 3, Овој закон влегува во сила осмиот ден од денот на објавувањето во "Службен весник на Република Македонија".
