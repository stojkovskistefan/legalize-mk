## AGREEMENT ON CULTURAL COOPERATION BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF NORTH MACEDONIA AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF TURKIYE

The Government of the Republic of North Macedonia and The Government of the Republic of Tiirkiye (hereinafier referred to as "the Parties"), Desiring to strengthen the established cultural relations and to promote the cultural cooperation based on mutual respect and understanding, Have agreed on the following:

##### ARTICLE 1

The competent authorities responsible for the implementation of this Agreement are; a) For the Government of the Republic of the North Macedonia: The Ministry of Culture b) For the Government of the Republic of Ttirkiye: The Ministry of Culture and Tourism,

##### ARTICLE 2

The Parties shall support the development of the cultural relations between the two countries a) intensifying the exchange of information about the cultures of the two countries b) encouraging and strengthening contacts, direct cooperation and exchange of experts: between institutions and individuals working in the field of culture in both countries ¢) Implementation of other measures to support research in all areas of culture 1 n ) 10 0414

## ARTICLE »

The Parties shall cooperate in the field of literature, publishing activity and translation, music and music-stage activity, drama activity, art and gallery activity, museum and library activity, in the area of protection and promotion of tangible and intangible cultural heritage, as well as in other segments of culture.

The Parties shall also cooperate in the field of cinematography and support mutual participation in international film festivals organized in their respective countries.

##### ARTICLE 4

The Parties shall support the participation of their representatives in congresses, symposia, seminars, conferences, competitions and other cultural events organized by the other Party.

## ARTICLE §

The Parties shall promote cooperation between the UNESCO National Commissions of both countries within the framework of UNESCO programs and initiatives of mutual interest, as well as cooperation with other international organizations whose domain is culture.

##### ARTICLE 6

l The Parties shall cooperate in preventing the illegal transferring and illicit trade of cultural property, in accordance with the 1970 UNESCO Convention on Means of Prohibiting and Preventing the Illicit Import, Export and Transfer of Ownership of Cultural Property.

##### ARTICLE 7

The Parties shall ensure effective protection of the rights for intellectual property transferred or created under this Agreement in accordance with their respective national legislation and international treaties to which the Parties are signatories. The meaning of the term "Intellectual Property” is established in Article 2 of the Convention Establishing the World Intellectual Property Organization, signed in Stockholm on July 14, 1967 11 04 14

##: ARTICLE &

The Parties shall mutually support the activities of the Cultural Center of the Republic of North Macedonia operating in Istanbul and the Yunus Emre Turkish Cultural Center operating in Skopje.

##### ARTICLE 9

The Parties shall support and promote the activities carried out by the citizens of the Republic of North Macedonia who graduated at the universities in Tiirkiye and the Turkish citizens who graduated at the universities in the Republic of North Macedonia in terms of cooperation between both countries.

The Parties shall encourage the development. of relations between diaspora of the Republic of North Macedonia and the Turkish diaspora in third countries and the efforts of both diasporas to develop cultural cooperation between the two countries.

##: ARTICLE 10

Activities arising from this Agreement shall be implemented through protocols between the institutions or organizations of the Parties. The protocols will set out the objectives, financial implications and other details related to specific projects of all Parties involved.

##### ARTICLE 11

Any difference or dispute arising from the interpretation or implementation of the provisions of this Agreement shall be resolved amicably through consultations and/or negotiations between the Parties.

##### ARTICLE 12

This Agreement may be amended by written consent of the Parties at any time. The amendments shall enter into force in accordance with the same legal procedure prescribed under the first paragraph of Article 13.

This Agreement shall enter into force on the date of the receipt of the last written notification, by which the Parties notify each other through diplomatic channels of the completion of the national legal procedures required for its entry into force.

This Agreement shall remain in force for period of one (1) year and it shall be renewed automatically for successive periods of one (1) year, unless one of the Parties notifies the other ! in writing through diplomatic channels of its intention to terminate the Agreement six (6) months prior to its expiration.
