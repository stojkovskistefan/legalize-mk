- the invitation of the President of the Host Party dated 17 January 2003, and the reply of the EU High Representative for Common Foreign and Security Policy on 28 January;

- that the purpose of the privileges and immunities as provided for in this Agreement is not to benefit individuals but to ensure the efficient performance of the EU operation;
