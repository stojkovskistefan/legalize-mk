## EQN E-SRAUD AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE REPUBLIC OF NORTH MACEDONIA AND THE PORTUGUESE REPUBLIC ON CO-OPERATION IN THE FIELD OF DEFENCE

The Republic of North Macedonia and the Portuguese Republic hereinafter referred to as “Parties”, Reaffirming their commitment to the principles of the Charter of the United Nations and the Organisation for Security and Co-operation in Europe;

Mindful of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) as a pillar of stability and security and recognizing the applicability of the provisions of the Agreement between the Parties to the North Atlantic Treaty regarding the Status of their Forces (NATO N SOFA), done in London, on June 19, 1951;

Desiring to deepen and strengthen the further development of the co-operation in defence and security areas;

Recognising that close military co-operation will contribute to the reinforcement of their collective and individual capabilities, to the security and integrity of their respective territories and to international peace and stability, Agree as follows:

##### ARTICLE 1

## PURPOSE

1. The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a framework for defence co-operation in accordance with International law, the respective internal law of the Parties and treaties that they have entered into.

2. The Parties shall identify and develop their Defence Co-operation bilaterally and in the framework of the North Atlantic Treaty, in pursuit of the principles and objectives set forth in the Preamble of this Agreement.

v

##### ARTICLE 2

## AREAS AND FORMS OF CO-OPERATION

1. The co-operation between Parties shall be developed in the following areas: a) Security and defence policy:

b) Peacekeeping and humanitarian operations — NATO, United Nations and European Union (EU) context;

¢) Establishment of programmes of instruction and joint training:

d) Co-operation on EU’s Common Security and Defence Policy and associated initiatives;

¢) Defence industries;

f) Environmental protection in the military locations:

g) Cyber Defence;

h) Gender issues and the role of women in the Armed Forces.

2. The Parties may agree on any other areas of mutual interest within the field of defence. in the ambit of this Agreement.

~~ 3. The Parties may identify and employ a variety of forms to promote the development of their objectives in the ambit of this Agreement.

4. If necessary, separate Memoranda of Understanding or Technical Agreements may be prepared and negotiated by both Parties, in accordance with and in support of the implementation of the provisions of this Agreement.

##### ARTICLE 3

## ANNUAL PLANS AND CO-OPERATION PROGRAMMES

The Parties shall co-ordinate, develop and adopt annual plans or co-operation programmes to establish the groundwork for co-operation.

##### ARTICLE 4

## FINANCIAL ASPECTS

1. The expenses derived from the implementation of this Agreement shall be borne by each Party in accordance with its respective applicable law.

2. The exchange of delegations of the Parties shall be made on the basis of reciprocity and having regard to the following provisions:

a) The sending Party shall bear the expenses for international transportation. accommodation and food and also those related with the daily allowance and other expenditures, such as personal items or communication-related expenses; . b) The receiving Party shall bear the expenses for transportation on its own territory, catering at the place of activity, as well as basic medical services in emergency cases.

3. The Parties may agree on different cost sharing for specific activities.

##### ARTICLE 5

## PROTECTION OF CLASSIFIED INFORMATION

The protection of Classified Information to be exchanged between the Parties shall be regulated by an Agreement on Mutual Protection of Classified Information in force between the Parties.

##### ARTICLE 6

## COMPETENT AUTHORITIES

1. The competent Authorities to implement this Agreement are:

a) For the Party of North Macedonia: the Ministry of Defence of the Republic of North Macedonia.

b) For the Portuguese Party: the Ministry of National Defence of the Portuguese Republic:

2. The Competent Authorities shall notify each other on their respective points of contact.

##### ARTICLE 7

## RELATION WITH OTHER INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENTS

The provisions of this Agreement shall not affect the rights and obligations deriving from other international agreements that are binding upon both Parties

##### ARTICLE 8

## ENTRY INTO FORCE

This Agreement shall enter into force thirty (30) days from the date of the receipt of the last written notification by which the Parties shall inform each other, through diplomatic channels, of the completion of the internal procedures required for that purpose.

## ARTICLE Y SETTLEMENT OF DISPUTES

Any dispute concerning the interpretation or application of this Agreement shall be settled through negotiations between the Parties through diplomatic channels.

##### ARTICLE 10

## AMENDMENTS

~~ 1. This Agreement may be amended by request of one of the Parties.

2. The amendments shall enter into force in accordance with the terms specified in Article 8.

##### ARTICLE 11

## DURATION AND TERMINATION

1. This Agreement shall remain in force for five (5) years and will be automatically extended for successive periods of five (5) years.

2. Either Party may, at any time, terminate this Agreement upon a prior notification to the other Perty in writing through diplomatic channels.

3. This Agreement shall cease ninety (90) days after the date of receipt of such notification.

4. The termination of this Agreement shall not affect the ongoing activities under this Agreement. unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing through diplomatic channels,

5. In case of termination of this Agreement, the implementation agreements. memoranda of understanding. protocols and arrangements as well as the annual plans referred to in Article 3 shall cease to be in force, except from the ongoing activities under them, unless otherwise agreed.

## REGISTRATION

Upon the entry in force of this Agreement, the Party in whose territory this Agreement is signed shall transmit it to the Secretariat of the United Nations for registration, in accordance with Article 102 of the Charter of the United Nations and shall notify the other Party of the completion of this procedure as well as of its registration number. Done in Skopje on the 11" of September of 2023 in two originals in the Macedonian, Portuguese and English languages, all texts being equally authentic. In case of divergence of interpretation, the English text shall prevail.
